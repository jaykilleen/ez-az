Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

EZ-AZ (ez-az.net) is a family friendly video game store run by Jay Killeen in Gumdale, Queensland, Australia. Kids and families build games, and we put them on the shelf for everyone to play. This privacy policy explains what information we collect when you visit the store and how we use it.

What we collect

We collect a small amount of information to make the store work:

  • Gamer name — when you play a game and make the leaderboard, you choose a name to display alongside your score. You pick this yourself. It does not have to be your real name.
  • Game scores — your high scores are saved so they can appear on the leaderboard inside each game.
  • Session token — we store a random token in your browser so the store remembers you across pages during your visit. This is not linked to any account or personal information unless you choose a gamer name.
  • Game submission details — if you submit a game to the store, we collect the name you provide and the game file itself. We do not require an email address.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information unless you choose to provide it.

How we use it

The information we collect is only used to run the store:

  • Gamer names and scores are shown on in-game leaderboards
  • Session tokens let your phone stay connected to the TV screen during multiplayer games
  • Submitted games are reviewed before being added to the shelf

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not sell or share it with anyone outside EZ-AZ.

Cookies and local storage

EZ-AZ uses browser session storage and local storage to keep the store running smoothly. This includes your session token and locally cached leaderboard scores (so the game still works if the server is temporarily unreachable).

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Children's privacy

EZ-AZ is built for families with kids. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Gamer names displayed on leaderboards are chosen by the player — we encourage kids to use a fun nickname instead of their real name.

If you believe a child has shared personal information through the store that you'd like removed, please contact us and we'll delete it promptly.

Third-party services

EZ-AZ uses the following external services:

  • Google Fonts — the store loads display fonts from Google's font service. Google's privacy policy applies to this request.
  • Honeybadger — we use Honeybadger to monitor for server errors. It receives anonymised error information (no personal data) to help us keep the store running.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook tracking, advertising networks, or any other tracking services.

Data storage

EZ-AZ is hosted on servers in the United States. If you are visiting from outside the US, information you submit (such as a leaderboard score) will be transferred to and stored on those servers.

Leaderboard entries are kept for as long as the game is on the shelf. Session tokens expire when you close your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us to remove any leaderboard entry or submitted game associated with you at any time. Contact us at the email below.
